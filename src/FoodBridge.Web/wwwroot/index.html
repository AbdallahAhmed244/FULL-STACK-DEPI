How FoodBridge Works

How FoodBridge Works

A streamlined, secure, and real-time platform for food redistribution — from posting to pickup in minutes.

1

Provider Posts Surplus

Restaurants, bakeries, and hotels list available food with details — quantity, pickup time, allergens, storage conditions, and photos.

2

Charity Reserves Food

Verified charities browse available donations, filter by category and date, and submit a reservation request — all in real time.

3

Pickup & Delivery

Provider confirms pickup details. Charity collects the food and marks the donation as completed. Both parties leave mutual reviews.

Who Uses FoodBridge?

Two types of organizations — united by one mission.

Food Providers

Restaurants, hotels, bakeries, and catering companies with daily food surplus that would otherwise go to waste.

  • Post food listings with photos & allergens
  • Accept or reject charity reservation requests
  • Confirm exact pickup location and time
  • Track donation history and impact stats

Charities & NGOs

Food banks, shelters, community centers, and relief organizations serving underprivileged communities.

  • Browse available food by category and date
  • Reserve donations with one click
  • Receive real-time notifications via SignalR
  • Track food received and submit partner reviews

Platform Features

Built with enterprise-grade technology to ensure security, reliability, and real-time coordination.

Real-Time Notifications

Instant SignalR push alerts for reservation updates, acceptances, pickups, and reviews.

JWT + Role-Based Auth

Secure JWT authentication with Admin, Provider, and Charity role-based access control.

Email Verification

All new accounts must verify their email before accessing protected features.

Concurrency Protection

Optimistic concurrency (RowVersion) prevents two charities from reserving the same listing simultaneously.

Admin Audit Trail

Every admin action is logged immutably — verifications, suspensions, removals, and resolutions.

Auto-Expiry Background Service

Hosted background service automatically marks listings as Expired when the pickup deadline passes.
